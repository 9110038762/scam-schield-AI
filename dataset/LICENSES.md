# Dataset Licenses & Attribution

ScamShield AI compiles and evaluates four primary publicly available research datasets for educational and academic research under strict academic integrity. Below are the original license declarations and attribution information.

---

- **Original Source:** [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection)
- **DOI:** 10.24432/C5CC84
- **Authors:** Tiago A. Almeida and José María Gómez Hidalgo
- **Year:** 2011
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **License Summary:** You are free to share (copy and redistribute) and adapt (remix, transform, and build upon) the material for any purpose, even commercially, under the terms of providing appropriate credit and indicating if changes were made.
- **Original Records:** 5,574 SMS messages (Ham: 4,827, Spam: 747)

- **Curator:** Anmol Shrivastav
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** Apache License 2.0
- **License Summary:** Grants users a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, and distribute the work.
- **Original Records:** 2,272 entries (Ham: 1,377, Spam: 895)
- **Note:** Contains a combination of real Indian telecommunication spam/scam messages and synthetic/augmented fraud examples.

## 3. Hinglish & English Financial Scam/Fraud Text Dataset
- **Original Source:** [Hugging Face: bolewara/hinglish-scam-text-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bolewara/hinglish-scam-text-dataset)
- **Curator:** Bolewara
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

- **Languages:** English, Hindi, and Hinglish

## 4. Indian Cyber Scam PhoneCall Hinglish Dataset
- **Original Source:** [Hugging Face: ysangam/Indian_Cyber_Scam_PhoneCall_Hinglish_Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ysangam/Indian_Cyber_Scam_PhoneCall_Hinglish_Dataset)

## Terms of Research Use
The data incorporated into the merged ScamShield corpus is exclusively utilized for experimental evaluation, research model benchmarking, and privacy-preserving scam detection demonstration. All raw data files are preserved intact in their original formats within `dataset/raw/`.
